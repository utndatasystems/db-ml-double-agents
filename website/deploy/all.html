Which papers count?

Full main-conference papers at SIGMOD/PODS, VLDB, ICDE, CIDR and EDBT are compared with main-conference papers at NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR. The comparison uses all publication years in the snapshot.

Workshops, companion proceedings and ordinary journal articles are excluded. PVLDB and PACMMOD are included as conference publication channels. Paginated DB papers need at least six pages; unpaginated CIDR papers also qualify. Known tutorials, demonstrations, editorials and reports are excluded. Missing length evidence is excluded rather than assumed.

The service fallback requires DB papers in two distinct publication years and qualifying PC service in two distinct years within 2022–2025. Multiple venues or roles in the same year still count as one service year.

ML-focused uses the reverse two-to-one rule. Mixed meets neither ratio. Both need five qualifying papers across three publication years. Limited evidence means neither the publication minimum nor the DB-focused service fallback is met.
